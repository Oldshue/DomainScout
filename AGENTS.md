# DomainScout dogfood contract

- Treat Hamp's requests to dogfood end to end as durable product work: reproduce the real user flow, fix encountered product defects in scope, deploy through the owned release lane, and verify the result. Do not stop at source-complete work.
- Every installed DomainScout launch must converge to and prove the current production release before serving the UI. A commit marker, app-bundle plist, updater receipt, or healthy process is not sufficient by itself: verify the installed tracked-file manifest against the desired immutable release, repair drift automatically, and only then start the local server and render the app.
- DomainScout production release acceptance is fleet-wide, never workstation-local. The owned production fleet is the `mac mini` and `MacBook` AgentForge execution targets. A release remains incomplete until both devices independently report the same immutable release-channel commit, a verified tracked-file manifest, matching app-bundle `BuildCommit`, a valid signature, healthy desktop readiness, and a successful unattended updater cycle. Keep the supported background updater installed on both devices so an offline device converges when it returns; never infer fleet convergence from the machine used to build or deploy the release.
- “All extensions”, “whole universe”, and equivalent language mean every extension currently accessible to the configured zone-data account. Report the accessible-zone denominator, successes, failures, snapshot dates, and whether the result is complete. A partial or stale subset must be labeled as a preview or lower bound, never 100% complete.
- Every results row must arrive with a populated numeric extension count equal to the cardinality of its concrete materialized extension list. Never render blanks, ellipses, estimates, lower-bound glyphs, “known” caveats, or a per-row completeness wait in the extension-count column. Whole-root verification is separate metadata; its absence must not suppress the real indexed count.
- Every rendered extension count, including zero, must remain a usable evidence control. Clicking it must immediately show exactly the concrete extensions counted by that number, then atomically update both the list and number if stronger evidence is merged. Preserve the precomputed zone-index path so rendering a page never launches whole-root checks for visible rows.
- Keep extension breadth and trend velocity distinct. Breadth is how many extensions currently contain a string. A trend requires dated evidence that the string was newly observed across multiple extensions in a daily zone diff, with the source and exact TLD set visible when retained.
- Track both exact-string velocity and vocabulary-free repeated fragments within distinct newly registered names. Discover fragments from the registration corpus itself; do not use regexes, dictionaries, curated industry terms, or motivating keywords to decide what can trend. Rank independent distribution and varied name contexts above mirrored multi-TLD product batches. Preserve click-through evidence for every actual name and TLD behind the signal; never present an opaque score.
- Search result ordering must be deterministic and re-applied when extension evidence changes. Default extension ranking is descending, with unknown values last.
- Product, company, or publication name crowding is affirmative demand evidence in every DomainScout investment analysis: multiple independent active commercial uses demonstrate buyer comprehension, category interest, and potential acquisition budgets. Never exclude, downgrade, or omit a domain merely because multiple products use the word or phrase. Keep demand validation separate from legal-risk review; only distinct evidence of a dominant exclusive right, likely confusion, or a materially unusable resale path may count against a candidate, and the report must preserve both the positive demand evidence and that separately sourced countercase.
- A positive selected-TLD market facet must render fresh explicit positives immediately as a truthful lower bound; it must never await or launch a whole-inventory registration sweep from the interactive desktop path. Negative or completeness claims still require a fresh complete-universe receipt. Run optional full-board verification only as isolated maintenance that cannot starve desktop readiness.
- Any shared research primitive must remain provider- and keyword-neutral. Tests must include an unrelated prefix fixture so motivating strings such as `agent` are not hard-coded.
- Unattended registry research must run independently of a user's laptop. On Railway or another ephemeral host, stream zone data and persist corpus evidence plus completion receipts to the configured S3-compatible evidence store; never attempt to fit the all-zone SQLite index into the 5 GB service volume.
- A cloud corpus may replace the published latest pointer only after every accessible source succeeds with zero failures. Partial runs remain inspectable by run ID but must never masquerade as the current complete universe. Apply the same receipt rule to unrelated trademark and security-monitoring corpora.

- Sale inference must distinguish a domain storefront migration from buyer use. Hamp confirmed on 2026-09-05 that IvyLake redirects are owner lander moves, not off-market sales. Apply the reusable storefront/content classifier to new probes AND all persisted ledger sources; never resurrect contradicted entries through seed precedence or retained history. Generic RDAP last-change dates, transfer locks, matching titles, DNS changes and mail setup cannot independently establish a sale. Expose explicit pending registrar transfer separately from inferred sales; retain exclusions and dated evidence. No perfect-market coverage or numerical accuracy claim without measured ground truth.

- Hamp's 2026-09-05 Sale Watch objective is to watch movements and reconstruct unreported sales; reported-sale summaries are not useful and must not dominate the working view. Feed the existing daily zone-delegation movement tape into the reconstruction queue, preserve the prior seller nameservers and dated before/after observations, and keep following suspected sales and pending transfers. Never classify a suspected move as a completed sale or stop probing merely because a heuristic fired.

- macOS can retain OS-managed com.apple.provenance after an attempted cleanup. Attribute presence alone is not evidence of a broken app and must not trigger rollback. Keep quarantine and unrelated attributes intact; require actual signing, source-manifest, launchd and live readiness verification. The September 5 MacBook rollback loop exposed this incorrect metadata gate.

- Sale Watch must order by the displayed DNS departure date, newest first, with unknown dates last and domain as the stable tie-breaker. Evidence tier and fresh probe timestamps must not silently reorder the chronology. Verify mixed transfers/acquisitions in the rendered cloud page.

- Investor alpha requires end-user adoption evidence, not merely a responding host. The September 5 live top-signal audit found unrelated branding, Loading, Placeholder, French construction and welcome pages incorrectly promoted. Re-adjudicate stored evidence as well as fresh probes; require matching destination branding and operating use for acquisition labels, retain uncertain moves/registrar transfers for follow-up, and treat ParkLogic origins as parking rather than proof of a sales lander. Never count generic traffic/SEO repurposing or fresh probe timestamps as retail demand trends.
- Hamp clarified that Sale Watch targets likely end-user purchases while names are moving, not confirmed transactions. Do not require a public report, known price, identified buyer or launched website to surface a promising early seller-departure/transfer lead. Keep those leads prominent and following up; reserve stronger adoption labels for corroborated evidence and keep migration/parking countercases visible.
